    A letter from the front room

    Dear parents, I started Ilm Ghar in 2017 at our dining table with four O Level students and one whiteboard. Ilm Ghar means house of learning, and it still is one.

    We keep batches to twelve because I want to know each child’s weak chapter by the third week. Every month you get a one-page report with their test marks and what we are working on next.

    Saima Qureshi, founder, teaches O and A Level Chemistry

    Questions

    Can my child try a class first?

    Yes, the first class of any batch is free. Message us to book a seat.

    Are classes for boys and girls together?

    Matric batches are separate. O and A Level batches are mixed, with separate seating.

    What if my child misses a class?

    Notes go on the batch WhatsApp group the same evening, and they can sit in another batch of the same subject that week.

    Do you prepare for Cambridge past papers?

    Every O and A Level batch does a timed past paper every second Saturday, marked by the next class.

    Is there parking?

    There is space for drop-off on the street outside House 12. Please don’t park across the neighbour’s gate.

    Do you teach online?

    No. Every class is in person, in the house.
