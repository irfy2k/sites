Baked at home, one day ahead

Dough & Daughters started at Eid in 2021, when Ammi’s fudge cake orders outgrew the family WhatsApp group. Her daughters, Hira and Mahnoor, run the orders and the cookies now.

Everything is baked the night before it’s delivered, in one home kitchen, in small batches. That is why we need your order a day ahead, and why we stop taking orders at forty a day.

First you choose.

Pick from the menu above, or send a picture of the cake you want.

Then you message us.

Your order arrives on WhatsApp with the items already typed. We confirm the price.

We bake overnight.

Pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or cash on delivery.

It arrives by four.

Delivery across DHA and Gulberg between 12 and 4 pm. Rs 300 for delivery.

What people ask before ordering

How far ahead do I order?

One day. Custom cakes need three days.

Do you deliver outside DHA and Gulberg?

For orders above Rs 5,000 we deliver to Model Town, Johar Town and Cantt.

Are your cakes eggless?

The fudge cake and the date cookies can be made eggless. Mention it in your order.

Can I pick up?

Yes, from Phase 5 between 11 am and 7 pm. We send the pin on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer or cash on delivery.

Do you make Eid and wedding boxes?

Yes, from 20 boxes. Message us two weeks ahead.
