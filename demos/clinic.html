We opened in Johar Town because families here kept driving to Gulberg for a check-up that takes twenty minutes.

Every visit starts with a written plan and the fee for each step. Nothing is done that you haven’t agreed to first.

If you’re nervous, tell us when you book. We’ll give you the first slot of the day, when the clinic is quiet.

Dr. A. Rahman

BDS, 12 years in practice
