Questions

I already have Instagram. Why a website? Google shows websites, not Instagram posts, when someone searches “bakery near me” or “dentist in Johar Town”. A website also works for people who don’t use Instagram.

Do I pay every month? No. One page and Business are hosted free. You pay once, plus a yearly domain if you want your own address.

Can it be in Urdu? Yes. English, Urdu, or both on the same page.

Who owns the site? You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you get the files.

What if I don’t like it? You see it before you pay the second half. Two rounds of changes are included.

How do customers order? Each item can have its own WhatsApp button that opens a chat with the item already typed.

Will I show up on Google? I link the site to your Google Maps listing and set it up for search. Ranking first for a busy term takes longer and isn’t promised.

What do you need from me? Your logo if you have one, three to six photos, your services or menu with prices, timings, address and WhatsApp number.
